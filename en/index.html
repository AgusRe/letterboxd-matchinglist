Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions about Letterboxd Matcher

Straightforward answers about comparing watchlists and choosing group movies.

How do I compare Letterboxd watchlists with friends? ▼

Type your friends' Letterboxd usernames in the input fields above and click Compare Lists. Letterboxd Matcher fetches public watchlists through secure CORS proxies and displays all movies matching across all profiles.

Do I need a Letterboxd Pro account or to sign in? ▼

No, you don't. Letterboxd Matcher is 100% free and open. You don't need a Pro subscription, login credentials, or an API key. You just need the profiles or lists to be publicly visible on Letterboxd.

Can I compare custom public lists in addition to Watchlists? ▼

Yes! Simply select the "Specific List" mode above and paste the full Letterboxd list URL (for example: https://letterboxd.com/username/list/list-name/). You can compare custom curated lists made by you or any community member.

What is the Top 5 Eliminator and how does it work? ▼

The Top 5 Eliminator is an interactive game mode to resolve movie night stalemates. The app randomly selects 5 movies from your common pool. Friends take turns clicking or tapping to eliminate films until 1 final winning movie remains with a celebratory confetti blast.

How do I export common movies to Excel or CSV? ▼

After running a comparison, click the Export CSV button in the results header. A clean CSV spreadsheet encoded with UTF-8 BOM will download immediately, ensuring accurate display of special characters in Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, and Apple Numbers.
